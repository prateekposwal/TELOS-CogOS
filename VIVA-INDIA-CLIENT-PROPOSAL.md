## 1. Project Overview

### 1.1 Partnership Summary

VIVA India is a trusted India tour operator with 13+ years of experience, ASTA certification, and a 5.0 Google rating. Despite these strengths, the digital presence has significant technical debt, duplicate content issues across three domains, and an outdated design that underserves the brand's premium positioning.

This proposal covers two parallel projects to **recover organic lead generation** and **establish a world-class luxury brand experience** across all VIVA India properties.

### 1.2 Project 1: SEO & Lead Generation Recovery

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | vivaindia.com, vivaindia.com.mx, vivaindia.com.co |
| **Focus** | Technical SEO fixes, content creation, link building |
| **Goal** | Recover and grow organic lead generation across all three markets |
| **Duration** | 3 months (ongoing maintenance available) |
| **Primary KPIs** | Organic traffic growth, lead form submissions, keyword rankings |

**Core Problem:** All three domains serve near-identical content with no canonical cross-referencing, causing Google to treat them as competing sources. This cannibalization, combined with missing schema markup, thin content, and no link-building strategy, has driven a measurable lead decline.

### 1.3 Project 2: Luxury Branding & UX Redesign

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | vivaindia.asia, vivaindia.org, lujoviajeaindia.com |
| **Focus** | UX redesign, luxury branding, premium positioning |
| **Goal** | Create a world-class luxury travel brand experience |
| **Duration** | 3–4 months |
| **Primary KPIs** | Brand perception, conversion rate, average booking value, time-on-site |

**Core Problem:** The current website uses an outdated WordPress theme (`tourpackage-v1-01`) with no luxury signals, poor mobile UX, and a visual identity that doesn't reflect the premium experience VIVA delivers on the ground.
