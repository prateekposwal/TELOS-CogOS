**Thursday — Refine (Prateek Reviews)**

**Friday — Celebrate**

## Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

### Pitfall 1: Trying to Learn Everything at Once
**What happens**: You read 10 guides in one day and retain nothing.
**Fix**: One topic per week. Go deep, not wide. Prateek will keep you focused.

### Pitfall 2: Perfectionism
**What happens**: You spend 3 hours on a meta description instead of 10 minutes.
**Fix**: Good enough today beats perfect next week. Submit your work to Prateek and let him tell you what to improve.

### Pitfall 3: Ignoring the Basics
**What happens**: You jump to advanced tactics before mastering fundamentals.
**Fix**: Trust the 12-week plan. Each week builds on the last. The basics are the foundation — don't skip them.

### Pitfall 4: Not Practicing Enough
**What happens**: You read everything but do nothing.
**Fix**: Every week has a hands-on task. Do it. Reading without doing is just entertainment.

### Pitfall 5: Being Afraid to Ask
**What happens**: You get stuck and waste hours instead of asking Prateek.
**Fix**: Prateek is your mentor. That's literally his job. Ask early, ask often, ask without shame.

### Pitfall 6: Comparing Yourself to Experts
**What happens**: You see someone with 10 years of experience and feel inadequate.
**Fix**: Prateek had 10 years of experience once too. Everyone starts somewhere. You're on Week 4, not Year 4. Keep going.

### When It Gets Tough

- **Week 3-4**: Technical SEO can feel overwhelming. Remember — you're a developer. You already understand this stuff. You just need the SEO vocabulary.
- **Week 6-7**: Writing content can feel vulnerable. It's not. It's just words on a page. Prateek will help you improve. That's what mentors do.
- **Week 9-10**: Link building outreach can feel discouraging when people don't respond. That's normal. It's a numbers game. Keep going.
- **Week 12**: Spanish content might feel impossible if you don't speak the language. That's okay. Translation tools exist, and the SEO structure is what matters most right now.
