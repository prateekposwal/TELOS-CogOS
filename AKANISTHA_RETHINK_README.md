## 1) STRUCTURAL READ — what actually changed

**The old model (ready-inventory) silently assumed:** a finished villa exists → ≥50% immersion→site-visit, ≥40% site-visit→booking, ≥35% booking→close, 2–5 closes/yr, trust surface = *physical property existence*. All were [R1] model assumptions, now structurally void: **you cannot convert a site visit into a close when there is nothing standing to visit.**

**The distinction that unlocks the rethink:** the ~10–25% off-plan discount (Savills / Dubai prelaunch economics) applies to **speculative off-plan units** — investors buying a box on a plan. A **bespoke private commission is the opposite**: the client pays AT PARITY or a **premium** to a finished villa *because they buy commitment, control, and outcome* — not a construction bet. Off-plan discount economics therefore do NOT transfer; branded-residence premium economics (global ~33% Savills, ~64% Dubai/CBRE) DO, **provided the trust surface is the brand + design-in-commitment + clinical forward-contract rather than a standing building.**

**Canceled vs preserved:**
- **CANCELED:** the three-stage ready-inventory funnel (immersion→site-visit→booking) as the conversion engine.
- **CANCELED:** personae "racing to buy ~20 ready sanctums"; the 8 kill-criteria + 90-day branch that assumed ready units.
- **PRESERVED:** the Advisory-Desk × Immersion distribution spine; the operator-decoupled clinical/retainer model (RIDEA — *return follows the operator, not the building*); GWI 5-criteria certification spine; the GWI 0.773 north star.

**Chosen coherent structure (discriminated below, Section 2):** **land-first book-then-build on the Narendra Nagar footprint**, sold as a private-commission + certified clinical outcome, with the Immersion reframed from "site visit" to **"underwriting / co-creation workshop."**

**The anchor moment for "₹80 Cr before walls exist"** is **the paid clinical baseline (stage 4)**: the client has already transacted for a measurable longevity *outcome* before committing to the build. **Revised composite funnel to instrument:** doors→immersion→land-orientation→studio→baseline→sign. Target **1–2 signed private commissions/yr across Narendra Nagar** by Y1–2 (model; down from the ready 2–5/yr — honest reduction because the build cycle lengthens).

## 5) RISK & REASSURANCE ARCHITECTURE (book-then-build trust surface)

The reassurance must substitute for "physical property exists." 4–6 mechanisms, each labeled **MODEL** (design projection) vs **FIELD-VERIFY** (legal/bank confirms before go-live):

1. **Milestone-staged payments + 70% escrow (MODEL / RERA-grounded).** 10% booking cap (§13(1)); remaining 90% split construction-linked (CLP) with symmetric interest clauses; ≥70% of collections held in designated escrow, released against land/construction only. RERA § 13(1) is the enforceable ceiling for the pre-agreement advance — the anti-ponzi guard.
2. **Completion guarantee instrument (FIELD-VERIFY).** Streets-of-stones/structural-warranty-style (cf. Buildiyo 10-yr structural warranty) via bank guarantee / surety; label: needs a bank's due-diligence sign-off before it is real.
3. **Exit / liquidity option (MODEL).** Staged assignment right pre-completion (mirrors ready-market early-resale mechanics and off-plan assignment channels) — gives the book-then-build buyer an off-ramp a ready buyer had via resale.
4. **Clinical/operator revenue covenant (MODEL).** The operator (separate clinical company, Sanctum Society retainers) covenant contributes a defined recurring stream; the buyer's "return follows the operator not the building" (RIDEA). This is the asset the buyer can value independently of the build completing.
5. **Design-in-escrow / IP lock (MODEL).** The bespoke design, BOQ (Buildiyo-style transparency), IEQ engineering spec, and clinical protocol are frozen in a signed commissioning document held in escrow — the buyer's IP is protected even if the build stalls.
6. **Insurance / bank instruments (FIELD-VERIFY).** Title insurance + construction-phase insurance; needs underwriter sign-off.

**Escrow-holder identity rule (from branded-residence failure-mode research):** the buyer must clearly see the escrow is held by a **neutral escrow/construction party — NOT the developer, and NOT the brand/operator.** "Trust the developer, not the branding" is the single most-critical reassurance lever for a ₹80 Cr no-walls purchase.
